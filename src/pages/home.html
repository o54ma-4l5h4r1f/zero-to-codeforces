From your first C++ program to Codeforces 1600.

A short path with a fixed order. Each lesson takes 10–15 minutes to read. Then you practice.

How to use this guide

  1. Go in order. Each lesson uses only the lessons before it. The last three lessons of Stage 3 are optional.
  2. Read, then solve. After each lesson, solve its practice ladder. Reading alone does not work.
  3. Think 20–40 minutes before you open a solution. Then write the code yourself, without looking.
  4. Mark it done and move on. Do not wait until you feel perfect.

What you find on a page

  • Arabic help. Hard words and sentences have a dotted line. Point at them, or tap them, to see the Arabic. The ع button at the top of the lesson list turns the sentence help off and on.
  • Animations. A picture with Next and Back buttons runs the code one step at a time. At each step, read the line under the picture before you press Next.
  • "why?" buttons. A small "why?" after a fact opens a short story. It tells where the fact comes from.
